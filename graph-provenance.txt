Graph and arithmetic certificates
Lean 4.19.0, mathlib v4.19.0

Entry point: GraphDefinition.lean, namespace CozeroGraph.

1. independentDominationPolynomial N z is defined without a factorization
   of N. Its finite sum runs through subsets of the ordinary representatives
   0,...,N-1. The membership test restricts each selected representative to
   a nonzero nonunit in ZMod N, checks independence, and requires that every
   vertex outside the set has a neighbor inside. Two vertices are adjacent
   when neither divides the other in ZMod N. The summand is z to the size
   of the selected subset.

2. all_sets_eq identifies this family of subsets exactly with the images
   of the m+1 divisor-grid cuts. The parameter is unique. The proof uses
   divisibility of actual residues, the two-row factorization of divisors
   of p^m*q, and the maximality of a cut in the product order. A fibre
   over divisor d has totient(N/d) elements. The sizes of the fibres and
   the sum over the two rows of each cut are proved in Lean.

3. graph_sum_eq connects the finite powerset definition to the cut
   polynomial. For arbitrary positive m, expressed in Lean as b+1,
   root_via_sections identifies every nonzero complex root with a zero of
   the reciprocal section. CozeroCardinality.graph_reciprocal supplies the
   stronger equality of evaluations on nonzero inputs.

4. exceptional_graph_roots gives the singleton {-m}. The theorem
   exceptional_graph_no_finite_limit excludes convergence to every complex
   number of every sequence of nonzero roots indexed by a strictly increasing
   subsequence of m. Both statements start from the powerset definition. No
   result from Rather, Fuchs, Mahler or a numerical computation is a hypothesis
   of these algebraic and exceptional-case theorems.

Provenance of these six source files
  CozeroChains.lean adapts a chain-weight certificate from an earlier
  calculation in an earlier local working directory. Its proofs were recompiled.
  A descriptive comment in the earlier copy counted the q-step switch
  index in the opposite direction. In weight(p,q,m,t), t is the number
  of p-steps AFTER the q-step. The change to the comment does not alter
  any definitions or proof terms.
  AlgebraBridge, DivisorGrid, ResidueModel, CutCardinality, and
  GraphDefinition were developed for the present verification.
  The ordinary mathlib lemmas used include ZMod.mul_inv_eq_gcd,
  ZMod.isUnit_iff_coprime, Nat.dvd_prime_pow, Nat.totient_div_of_dvd,
  Nat.totient_prime_pow_succ, Nat.totient_mul and Nat.totient_prime.

Build with the project-wide Lake instructions supplied with the supplement.
Preserve these unqualified module names when putting the files in a common
Lake library. No locally compiled .olean or project-specific absolute paths
are needed in a distribution. Each module prints the axiom dependencies of
its main results. Only Lean's standard foundations (propositional extensionality,
classical choice, and quotient soundness) occur across these results.
